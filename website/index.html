Bridge for Android and Mac.

Update all of the apps together, then pair again. They talk only on your Wi-Fi.

  • Codes

    SMS and notification codes are copied on the Mac. A sound can play when one arrives. History can be cleared.

  • Clipboard

    Copy on one device, paste on the other. Android also has a Quick Settings tile, a notification action, and Send to Bridge on selected text.

  • Calls

    An incoming call shows on the Mac. Answer or decline it from there. The card can be turned off in settings.

  • They find each other again

    If the Mac app gets a new address, the phone picks it up on the local network. You do not scan the QR again for that.

  • Settings

    Turn codes, clipboard, and calls off separately. Off means that data stops, not only that the control is hidden.

  • Session

    Pairing is a QR scan. The session key is derived on the devices. Replayed messages and unsigned discovery are ignored. Keys are not kept in a cloud backup.

  • Updates

    Each app can check for a newer build. The Mac app installs an update only after the download is on the allowlist and the signature matches.
